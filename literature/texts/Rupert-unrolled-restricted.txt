Matthew Rupert
Abstract
We consider the category of generalized weight modules over the unrolled restricted
H
quantum group U q (g) of a finite-dimensional simple complex Lie algebra g at root
of unity q. Although this category does not admit projective modules, it is filtered
by subcategories with enough projectives. We show that the projective covers of irreducible modules in these subcategories satisfy a variant of BGG reciprocity and give a
generators and relations description when g = sl2 .

Introduction

The unrolled quantum group UqH (g) associated to a finite-dimensional semisimple complex
Lie algebra g is an extension of the non-restricted specialization Uq (g) of De Concini and
Kac [DCK]. It can be obtained by taking a smash product with the universal enveloping
algebra of the Cartan subalgebra h of g, UqH (g) := Uq (g) ⋊ U(h). Inspired by previous
work of Ohtsuki [O], a quotient of this algebra called the unrolled restricted quantum group
H
U q (g) was introduced in [GPT1] for g = sl2 and studied at even roots of unity as an
example for constructing link invariants from its representation theory (see also [GPT2]).
1

Unrolled restricted quantum groups have since found substantial applications to quantum
H
topology as their category of weight modules Repwt U q (g) can be used to construct decorated
topological quantum field theories [BCGP, D, DGP]. A second application of these algebras,
and the application of primary concern for this article, is the logarithmic Kazhdan-Lusztig
correspondence for vertex operator algebras.
The study of equivalences between categories of modules over quantum groups and vertex
operator algebras began with the pioneering work of Kazhdan and Lusztig [KL1, KL2, KL3,
KL4]. Proved therein was a braided equivalence between categories of modules over simple
affine vertex operator algebras Lk (g) with k +h∨ ∈ Q≥0 where h∨ is the dual Coxeter number
and a corresponding quantum group. It is generally expected that many vertex operator
algebras (in particular, those which admit free field realisations) should admit categories of
modules which are equivalent to some category of modules over a corresponding quantum
group. This expectation is broadly referred to as the Kazhdan-Lusztig correspondence,
and logarithmic when the categories involved are non-semisimple. The study of logarithmic
Kazhdan-Lusztig equivalences was first explored for the Triplet vertex operator algebra W(p)
(p ≥ 2) in the work of Feigin et al [FGST1, FGST2] where a ribbon equivalence with a
certain category of modules of the restricted quantum group U q (sl2 ) was conjectured. This
conjecture was studied [NT, KS] and refined [CGR] to an equivalence between modules
over W(p) and a quasi-Hopf modification Ũq (sl2 ) of Uq (sl2 ). This refined conjecture was
eventually proven for p = 2 in [CLR] and for all p ≥ 2 in [GN].
The Triplet W(p) contains a sub-VOA called the Singlet M(p) which was expected to admit a category of modules which is braided equivalent to the category of weight modules
H
Repwt U q (g) over the unrolled restricted quantum group at 2p-th root of unity q. This
conjecture was motivated in [CMR, CGR] and connections to unrolled restricted quantum
groups were established for simple current extensions of vertex algebras related to the SinT
glet in [ACKR, CRR]. The conjecture was further refined to a particular category OM(p)
H
